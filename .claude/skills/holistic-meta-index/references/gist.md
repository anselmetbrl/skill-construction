# gist — selection, not authorship

> the S4 inversion. the old instruction — "fill these fields about the repo" — produced
> authorship: 0 of 75 claim-lines grounded in the test run. a field that must be filled
> WILL be filled. so the gist is now SELECTION: spans chosen from the frozen dossier,
> arranged. your contribution is which spans and in what order — not prose.

## the format forces the grounding

    every claim-line, no exceptions:

        <claim> ← "<verbatim span>" (<link>)

    or, when the material does not say:

        #void — <reason>

    a paraphrase without its span is authorship — forbidden. the claim may compress;
    the span beside it is what makes it a claim instead of a guess. trimming a quote
    with … is fine; twisting it is not; the link must reach the source.

## spine FIXED · leaves EARNED

    what-it-is    (notion)      what the project IS, in its own words
    why-it-is     (question)    the need it answers — readmes usually DO say this;
                                select it, don't invent it. unsaid → #void.
    how-it-is     (mediation)   technology   internal: what it is built of
                                technicality external: deps · stack-fit
    leaves        as many further grounded nodes as the repo actually YIELDS —
                  never padded to fill the shape. a thin repo is a FINDING:
                  say plainly that every leaf is #void, and stop.

## each angle is distinct

    readmes use their OWN headings — never assume `## Features` exists.
    never reuse one span across what/why/how. one span, one home.

## flags — impartial, each WITH its quote

    hype          a superlative asserted as fact in the project's own promotional
                  register — "the best/first/only/fastest" · self-benchmarks
    filler        seo/ai-routing text where substance should be
    abandonment   as dated FACTS (last release, maintainer silence), not a verdict
    contribution  fake/weak/inflated signals, quoted
    unfree?       paywall-sabotage — cloud hosting is fine; crippled self-hosting
                  is the flag
    contradiction claims that conflict ACROSS the repo — record both spans; the
                  user resolves
    trust is subtractive and the SUBTRACTION is the user's: list flags, never
    total them into a score.

## harvest — collect, do not relate

    while reading, every span that positions this repo against another NAMED thing:

        #graph-harvest
        "<verbatim span>" (<link>) → names <the string as written>

    "an X alternative" · "unlike X" · "drop-in replacement for X" · "incompatible
    with X" · "successor to X". resolution against the collection happens in the
    workbench, mechanically. deciding what it MEANS happens at the session, by the
    user. here you only collect.

## forbidden in a gist

    judgment adjectives outside quotes — mature · promising · legacy · impressive
    counts in prose (stars/forks/issues live in the glance line only)
    cross-repo statements — clusters, hinges, bridges, outliers are session material
    any sentence about the USER

## micro-rhythm

    one repo at a time, from its dossier alone. after each: run C1 on that gist —
    the count, not a feeling. commit every ~10.
