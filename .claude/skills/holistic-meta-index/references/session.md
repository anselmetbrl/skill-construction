# session — the co-construction checkpoint, where set-level judgment lives

> the pipeline's center of gravity. everything the old chain batch-authored — tags,
> relations, order, emergence — is RULED here by the user, over the workbench's
> exhibits. sensemaking happens within the user; you exhibit, propose, record.
> the session is not overhead on the pipeline. it IS the pipeline's point.

## how to walk it

    one exhibit-family per message. chunk within a family when it is long — the
    user is partially blind with limited attention; a wall harvests a sloppy "ok",
    and a harvested "ok" is a stolen ruling.
    suggested arc: E1 whole-view → E6 checks → E2 tags → E3 relations → E5 order →
    E7 emerge → E4 flags woven in where they bear. the user reorders at will.

## how to offer

    everything you put forward is [proposal], its grounding attached, in the same
    message. recommend a lead when the user seems unsure — never a bare menu, never
    a finished fact. mark your uncertainty honestly; it is signal, not weakness.

## how the user rules

    accept · edit · reject · add · defer — their words, recorded VERBATIM in
    rulings.md as they land, with what they ruled on. rulings are append-only;
    a changed mind is a new ruling superseding the old, shadow kept.

    the user's additions need no grounding — their judgment IS the warrant.
    exception, non-negotiable: if frozen material CONTRADICTS a ruling, surface
    the span once, plainly, then record whatever they decide. never silently
    comply, never argue past one surfacing.

## what gets ruled

    tags       the ≤12 set · each repo's ≤4 · #void where they say so. the caps
               discipline your proposals; the user may break a cap — record that
               they did. the cap is theirs.
    relations  each candidate edge: confirmed with its type, retyped, or dropped.
               a proposed THIRD type: adopted or refused — you never coin.
    order      the index order. default seed order; one word keeps it.
    emerge     which candidate observations are true enough to speak. none is valid.
    deferrals  an unruled item is recorded [deferred], rendered as #void, never
               quietly decided by you.

## pace

    the user's, absolutely. multiple sittings are normal — rulings.md is the
    resume-state; reopen where it stops. no summarising the user's own rulings
    back at them from memory: re-read rulings.md.

## exit

    every set-level item ruled or [deferred] → commit rulings.md → render.
