> **what this is.** a decoder for the etymological vocabulary in the tag block of
> `indexing_autoinfo_hg_v0.1.md` (its L232–357). it does NOT change that file — it
> sits beside it and distills the GENERATOR beneath the four stacked drafts.

> **the reframe.** this is not a repo-tag whitelist (that stays deferred). it is a
> generative meta-language: `term = prefix(es) · ROOT · suffix`. so this module heals
> the GRAMMAR — roots + affixes + rule — not the saturated word-dump. distilling words
> chases infinity; distilling the generator is finite.

> **read it as a scaffold to CORRECT.** the meanings below are STANDARD Latin etymology.
> your idiolect may assign a root a different essential sense — yours wins. `[evolve]`
> marks what's uncertain; `!` = essential and `?` = tentative are preserved from your source.
